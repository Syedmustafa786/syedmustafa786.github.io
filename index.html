02 · Approach

I help large, regulated organisations turn ambiguity into funded, shipped, measurable outcomes — not slide decks.

Currently embedded inside a national rail operator's innovation ecosystem as a portfolio lead, I shape and steer a multi-modal AI and data agenda spanning GenAI, Computer Vision, IoT, satellite imagery, Agentic AI, and immersive training. 04 · Method

The four moves behind every shipped venture.

Stage-gated.
Discovery-first.
Influence-led.
01 Discover

Validate the problem before the technology.

Structured as-is / to-be sprints, stakeholder interviews, process walk-throughs, and operational data analysis — before any commitment to build.

  • — Design Thinking
  • — Discovery sprints
  • — Process mapping
02 Frame

A funded business case, in business language.

Translate engineering constraints into commercial framing and executive ambitions into AI architecture decisions. Build / buy / partner analysis on every track.

  • — ROI framing
  • — Build/buy/partner
  • — C-level alignment
03 Deliver

Stage-gated PoC → pilot → pre-industrialisation.

Scope, milestones, risk register, budget framework. SAFe-6 PI cadence for portfolio coordination. Stage-gates ensure each phase delivers learning before the next.

  • — SAFe 6 APM · POPM
  • — Risk + budget governance
  • — Vendor orchestration
04 Hand off

Production readiness, not pilot purgatory.

Pre-industrialisation handover with clear criteria. Sunset under-performers. Reallocate capacity to what's working. 6 of ~32 PoCs scaled with 80%+ adoption.

  • — Handover governance
  • — Adoption tracking
  • — Kill decisions
